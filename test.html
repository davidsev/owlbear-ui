Form submission (setFormValue / reset)

A real <form> with no novalidate: Submit is blocked (with a native bubble) until Name is filled in, the values arrive in FormData under their name, and Reset restores the values authored in the markup. The fieldset disables its control without touching its disabled property, and disabled controls submit nothing.
